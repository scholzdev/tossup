# Tossup

For a development session with every character, coin, stage and collection entry unlocked, start with `TOSSUP_DEV=1 love .`. New runs begin with 5,000 gold. Development runs use a separate save; development unlocks are not written to the normal profile.

The wiki is also published automatically with GitHub Pages on every push to `main` (`.github/workflows/wiki.yml`; Settings > Pages > Source: GitHub Actions). `./tools/build_wiki.sh` builds a local wiki of all coins, chips, prizes, modifiers and characters into `wiki/` (open `wiki/index.html`; German pages in `wiki/de/`, with an EN / DE switch at the top right). `./tools/build_all.sh release [X.Y.Z] [--dry-run]` runs the tests, builds everything, tags `vX.Y.Z` and publishes the four downloads (Windows zip, macOS zip, Linux AppImage, .love) as a GitHub release; needs `brew install gh` and `gh auth login` once (`tools/release.sh`). `./tools/build_all.sh` builds every platform into `dist/<platform>/`: `love/Tossup.love`, `macos/Tossup.app`, `windows/Tossup-windows.zip` (with `Tossup.exe`), `linux/Tossup.AppImage`. Run `./tools/run.sh` (same as `love .`; the Dock then shows LÖVE) or `./tools/run.sh app` (checks the OS, builds the matching binary and launches it: `dist/macos/Tossup.app` with the right name and icon, the Linux AppImage, or the Windows exe). Detailed design and rules are in [docs/](docs/README.md). The first Play starts an interactive tutorial (also on the title menu: Tutorial). Keyboard: arrows move the focus, Enter presses, Space flips, D discards, 1-3 use chips, O opens the shop, Q/E change page or tab (Options > Controls lists everything). Controller: D-pad / stick to move, A to press, B or Start = Esc, X = flip, Y = discard, LB / RB / LT / RT = previous / next page, tab or character. The window can be resized. Esc opens the menu during a run (Options: Game tab with Clear Progress, Sound tab with master / music / effects sliders); F3 shows seed and RNG details.

## How a run plays

- **Goal:** each level has a quota of points. Flip coins to score points before your coins run out. A level lasts exactly as long as your stack: every coin is played once (no reshuffle), so the number shown at the top right is the coins left, and a bigger deck means more flips. The quota scales with your deck size (points per coin: 0.6 / 0.9 / 1.6 / 3.0). You start with 25 gold. There is no player HP: coins score points, and penalty effects raise the quota instead. Four levels; the last is The House (it inverts every 5th flip). When the stack is empty and the quota isn't met you can **exchange**: pay gold (10, then 15, 20 within a level) to put 3 of the coins you already played back into the stack, at most 3 times per level. If you can't afford an exchange, or have used all three, the level is lost.
- **Saving:** the run is autosaved at the start of every level and in the shop; Continue on the title menu resumes it, even after quitting. A level in progress restarts from its opening hand.
- **Stages:** each character has a difficulty stage 1 to 8 (play screen); a win on its highest unlocked stage unlocks the next (harder quotas, less gold, pricier shop, fewer exchanges; see docs/gameplay.md).
- **Characters:** the Blade is open; winning a run unlocks the Seer, winning with the Seer unlocks the Trader. Crashes are written to `crash.log` next to `runs.log`.
- **Coin sets:** before a run you pick a character and one of its three coin sets (up to 5 coins). Each set may contain at most 3 common, 2 uncommon, 1 rare and 1 epic coin in total. Edit sets in Coin Sets (title menu): click a coin to add it, click a slot to remove it, press Save Set. Blade and Trader start with 3 coins, leaving room for rarer unlocks; the deck grows through the shop.
- **Opening hand:** each level starts with up to 5 coins from your deck. Click coins to mark them, then press Discard to throw the marked ones away for the level (free; nothing marked means no discard). Start Level (or Space) begins.
- **Combo:** each flip with the same result as the one before raises a multiplier on its points and gold (x1.25 per extra step, up to x3); a different result resets it. Discard risky coins to protect a streak.
- **Coin bank:** the left panel shows the next 3 coins; the first is the one you play. The Discard button throws away only the coin in play, for the level (free; the last usable coin can't go); the other bank coins can't be picked. The bank refills from a random draw pile whenever fewer than 3 coins are left; the pile is never reshuffled. Flip with the Flip button or Space; the result shows, then its effect applies, then Next Coin continues.
- **Energy:** some coins cost energy to flip (shown on the coin, e.g. Hammer costs 2). You start each level with 3 energy; Copper, Spark and Flux Capacitor give some back. A coin you can't pay for can't be flipped, so discard it. The last usable coin always flips.
- **Endless Mode:** after the boss you can keep going: levels 5, 6, 7... ask 0.5 more points per coin each time and invert every 5th flip; the run ends when you lose one.
- **Meeting the quota:** the level stays open. You get the level payout at once, and every 2 points beyond the quota pay 1 more gold. Keep flipping for gold or press Open Shop (top right) whenever you like; running out of coins after the quota also goes to the shop. The boss ends the run the moment its quota is met.
- **Shop:** four coin offers (Coin), two consumables (Chip) and one relic (Prize), plus an odds tuner (+3% Heads for 10 gold) and coin removal for the coin selected in your deck. The left panel shows chips and prizes you own; hover an icon to see its effect. Reroll costs 4 gold, 2 more each time. The deck has 5 slots at the start; click the first dark slot in your deck to buy another for 5 gold (up to 10). A full deck can't buy coins until you buy a slot or remove a coin. **Buying a coin you haven't unlocked yet unlocks it permanently**, so it can go into your coin sets afterwards.
- **Chips** (items, up to 3 held) are used from the bar in the round while a coin is dealt: Force Heads/Tails, Weighted, Double Down, Swap, Peek, Extra Draw (a played coin returns to the pile; Lucky and Hourglass do the same). **Prizes** (relics) are passive for the run: Magnet, Lucky Penny, Broken Clock.

## Code layout

- `src/game.lua`, `src/rng.lua`, `src/profile.lua`, `content/`: rules, data and saved progress, no LÖVE dependency.
- `main.lua` forwards LÖVE callbacks to `src/ui/app.lua`. `src/ui/state.lua` is the shared UI state, `actions.lua` the click/flow logic, `draw.lua` and `theme.lua` the drawing helpers. `src/ui/views/` has one file per screen (`title`, `menu` = play screen, `sets`, `collection`, `options`, `encounter`, `shop`, `finish`).
- Progress (unlocked coins, coin sets, collection, options) is saved to `profile.lua` in the LÖVE save folder.

## Coin hooks

Each coin is `content/coins/<id>.lua`: data (`name`, `rarity`, `probability`, `heads`, `tails`, optional `energy_cost`) plus optional hooks. The full reference is the header of `src/hooks.lua`. Short form: `on_deal`, `on_flip` (change the outcome), `on_resolve` (edit the effect list before it applies), `on_discard`, `on_odds` (pure odds modifier), `grow(inst, "level"|"flip"|"discard")` for run-long scaling stored on the coin instance, and `register(ctx)` to subscribe to bus events (`src/signal.lua`). Relics use `register(ctx)` too (`src/relics.lua`); items are in `src/items.lua`. LÖVE runs LuaJIT, which has no `table.unpack`: copy lists by hand in game code.

## Tests and tools

- `lua tests/test_game.lua`, `test_hooks.lua`, `test_items.lua`, `test_bank.lua`, `test_profile.lua`.
- `lua cli.lua 381927 blade` replays one deterministic automated run.
- `lua tools/sim.lua` plays whole seeded runs headlessly with three bots (random, greedy, smart) and prints clear rates per level. Flags: `--runs N`, `--char blade|seer|trader`, `--bot random|greedy|smart`, `--set default` (start from the default deck instead of a best-coins 10-coin set), `--unlock all`, `--coins` (per-coin strength table), `--quota 15,22,28,40` and `--payout 20,25,30` (override the route), `--trace SEED` (full log of one run). Bots are crude: use the numbers to find broken coins and a broken economy, not to judge fun.
- `python3 tools/gen_title_scene.py` regenerates the main menu backdrop `assets/ui/title_scene.png` (pixel-art casino back room, drawn in code); `python3 tools/gen_coin_icons.py` regenerates the coin art in `assets/coins/`; `python3 tools/gen_ui_icons.py` regenerates the item, relic and UI icons in `assets/items`, `assets/relics`, `assets/ui` (both need Pillow). Fonts and the palette follow the reference project at `/Users/scholzf/dev/uni/secret`; the original design documents are [readme.md](readme.md) and [impl.md](impl.md).
